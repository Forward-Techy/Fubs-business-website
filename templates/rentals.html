How rental enquiries work

  1. Tell us the item, location and rental dates.
  2. We check partner availability and condition.
  3. You receive the price, deposit and rental terms.
  4. Booking proceeds after written confirmation.
Partner-led model

FUBS may coordinate rentals on behalf of asset owners. We will confirm the owner, availability, charges, deposit and responsibilities before any booking is accepted.
